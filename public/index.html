                    📚 How To Play (Live Game)
                    1. ✍️Type four tile numbers in chat, e.g. 2 5 8 12. Every round has 8 separate groups (some 2-level, some 3-level fusions). Only 24 tiles show at a time; when tiles fuse, new tiles drop into the free spaces until every group is found
                    2. 🧩If all four share a link (Ford, Toyota, Honda, BMW), they are destroyed and fuse into one new tile (“Car”) with a brand-new number
                    3. ⚡Fuse again: the new “Car” tile can now join Boat, Plane and Train to fuse into the final group “Transportation”. A 3-level group goes one step further: the new tile fuses into another new tile, which then fuses into the final group
                    4. 👀Three of four right shows “one away”. Anything else is marked wrong
                    5. ⭐Every fusion earns points — deeper levels are worth more (x1, x2, x3)
                    6. 🔥Fuse back-to-back for a combo streak: 1x, 2x, 3x, up to 4x. A wrong guess resets it
                    7. ⏰No time limit. The clock only shows how long each puzzle takes
